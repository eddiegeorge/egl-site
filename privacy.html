Software we develop and operate

We develop and operate software applications, including on behalf of clients. Some of these applications let users connect their accounts on third-party platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. When a user does so:

  • We access their platform data only after they authorise it through the platform’s own sign-in and consent screen, and only within the permissions they grant.
  • We use that data only to provide the service the user has asked for, such as showing their account and content performance and, where they choose, publishing content they have approved.
  • We do not sell the data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.
  • Access tokens and data are stored securely, encrypted, with access limited to the systems that provide the service.
  • We keep the data only as long as needed to provide the service, and in line with each platform’s terms. A user can disconnect an account at any time, in the application or in the platform’s own settings, and we then stop accessing it.
